GROOME Groome airport shuttle official schedule

Door-to-door van between the Grand Canyon (Tusayan IMAX) and Flagstaff — stopping at Flagstaff Pulliam Airport (FLG), the Amtrak depot, and downtown. Three round-trips daily · ~2 hr · 928-350-8466. Fares ~$49–$59 + $8 park fee · book ≥24 hr ahead.

Grand Canyon → Flagstaff (FLG)
Flagstaff → Grand Canyon

✈️ Flying out of Phoenix Sky Harbor (PHX)? Connect at Flagstaff to Groome's Flagstaff↔PHX line (every ~1–2 hr, ~2½ hr each way).

Times current as of June 2026 — schedules change seasonally, so confirm when booking.
